Other Events

Alongside our Sunday Practica, we’ve organised and collaborated on other tango events in Cambridge.

Milonga Allá Arriba

A social tango evening organised in collaboration with CamTango. In the past, these milongas have been held at St Paul’s Church on Hills Road, Cambridge, on selected Friday evenings.

Please check our Facebook group for any upcoming editions, locations, timings and prices.

Special events and visiting teachers

From time to time we host or collaborate on additional milongas, workshops and visits by guest teachers. We announce new events through our Facebook group.
