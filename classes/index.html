Classes

Classes at Sunday Practica

Our Sunday Practica typically includes a 2–3 pm improver/intermediate class for dancers with at least a little tango experience. Classes may be taught by us, local partners or visiting instructors.

Check the Practica page for the usual programme and the Facebook group for confirmed dates.

Private and small-group classes

These are perhaps the most effective way of polishing and enriching your dancing. Working with instructors one-on-one, or joining a small, gender-balanced group of four to six well-matched students, gives you plenty of feedback tailored to your needs and lots of helpful practice, all at your own pace.

Radu and Sylwia offer these on demand and tailor them to you and your requests. We have typically charged around £60/hour, depending on the session (for example, one or both of us, duration and location). Please confirm current pricing when enquiring.

Contact us through Facebook or rdjianu@gmail.com if interested.

Other opportunities to learn

If our schedules don’t work for you, have a look at the classes offered by our friends at Truly Tango and Simply Tango. They are wonderful teachers and we’ll be just as happy to meet you on the dance floor!
