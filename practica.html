Sunday Practica

Going deeper into tango, not just learning more of it.

Cambridge Tango Labs is a technical tango hub for Cambridge dancers who want to understand, refine and develop their dancing.

Rather than simply learning more moves, we explore how and why tango movement works, developing the technique and awareness that allow you to dance with greater clarity, control, connection and creativity.

The practicas are run by Ayna Milady de Winter (Instagram).

What we focus on

Our sessions explore the different elements that make tango work:

  • Technique & body mechanics: understanding how your body moves and how to use it efficiently
  • Movement quality: refining balance, posture, weight transfer, walking and pivots
  • Connection & embrace: developing clearer communication, comfortable connection and awareness of your partner
  • Musicality & timing: exploring how movement, pauses and dynamics respond to the music
  • Dissociation & coordination: developing the control and mobility needed for more complex movement
  • Creative movement: exploring how to adapt, combine and link movements rather than simply following fixed sequences
  • Individual refinement: getting feedback and working on the details that can make your dancing feel more comfortable, clear and expressive

The aim is not to collect more and more sequences, but to develop the tools that help you understand, adapt and create movement for yourself.

Who is Cambridge Tango Labs for?

Cambridge Tango Labs is aimed at tango dancers who have been dancing for around six months or more.

You don't need to be an advanced tango dancer. We still work on the fundamentals, but we approach them with the aim of developing and refining your dancing, rather than introducing tango from scratch.

If you're a complete beginner with no previous tango experience, our sessions probably aren't the best place to start. We'd recommend looking at the beginner-friendly tango classes and events running in Cambridge on Wednesdays and Thursdays, which are designed to introduce the fundamentals from the beginning.

Some basic tango etiquette rules are also worth reading before you join.

Once you've built up some basic experience, we'd love to have you join us at Tango Labs.

When & Where

We run practicas on the 1st and 3rd Sunday of each month, from 2–6pm.

We take breaks during the summer and Christmas holidays, so dates can vary.

For specific dates, teachers, topics and any changes to the schedule, please check our Facebook events.

You can also join the Facebook group to keep up with upcoming events and announcements.
