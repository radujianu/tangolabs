Our main event

Sunday Practica

Our relaxed Sunday afternoon of Argentine tango: a class, plenty of practice, music and good company. Whether you already dance or are just curious, come and meet the community.

Check before coming. We usually meet on the first, third and (where there is one) fifth Sundays of the month, with breaks around summer and Christmas. Please check our Facebook group for confirmed dates, venue details and any changes.

What happens?

2–3 pm

Improver/intermediate class

Suitable for anyone with at least a little tango experience. Classes are offered by us, Cambridge partners or visiting instructors.

3–5 pm

Practica / social dancing

Practise, dance or just chat. Our friendly crowd is happy to help newcomers take their first steps. We play mostly Golden Age tango in traditional tandas, and there is usually coffee and a few snacks.

New to tango?

You are welcome to come along to see what it’s like, meet people and try a few steps. You don’t have to know anyone beforehand.
